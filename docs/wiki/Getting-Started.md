# Getting Started

## Install

1. Download `Waypoint.apk` from [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/the-geeky-couldron/waypoint/releases/latest).
2. Open the APK and follow Android's installation prompts. Android may ask you to allow installation from the app you used to open the file.
3. Open Waypoint, choose a world and tap **Open map**.
4. Tap **Locate** and grant precise location when Android asks.

The current public download is a GitHub-sideloaded APK. Android Auto may not list a sideloaded Car App Library app in the car launcher; see [[Android Auto]].

## Choose a destination

- Search for a place using **Search places**.
- Long-press the map to place a pin.
- Choose a saved or recent place.
- Open a supported `geo:` or `google.navigation:` link. Waypoint asks before it resolves and previews the destination.

Pick **Car**, **Motorcycle**, **Bicycle** or **Walk**, review the route and tap **Start navigation**. While navigating, the map follows your position and shows the next turn, remaining distance and estimated arrival time. Waypoint can recalculate when the route is left.

## Find the main controls

- **Map** returns to the live map.
- **Worlds** searches, filters, previews and switches map styles. Changing the world does not end an active route.
- **Saved** keeps destinations on this device; saved names can be edited.
- **Quests** optionally tracks personal tasks and focus sessions. Quests never gate navigation or themes.
- **Settings** contains spoken guidance, units, map tilt, location status and data-clearing controls.
- **Map info** opens the selected world's key for its POI symbols.

## Day, night and 3D

The phone's local clock selects the map's day treatment from 06:00 to 18:59 and its night treatment from 19:00 to 05:59. It checks for a change once per minute. The 2D/3D control changes camera tilt in every world. Dark Zone also has its own flat tactical map and holographic 3D treatment.

## Voice

Waypoint uses the Android text-to-speech engine selected on the device. When **World voice style** is enabled, each world applies its own bounded pitch/rate profile and original introductory, en-route and arrival phrases. Route turns and road names remain first; flavor phrases are suppressed near imminent turns. Waypoint does not bundle audio recordings from third-party packs. See [[Navigation and Voice]] and [[Sources and Licensing]].
