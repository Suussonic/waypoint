# Artwork and map-preview sources

Waypoint's 27 portrait world illustrations in `app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/art_*` are original images generated for this project with `gpt-image-2` and selected for legibility in the mobile gallery. The retained cover prompts are in [`artwork-prompts-v2.jsonl`](artwork-prompts-v2.jsonl); raw source generations and older unused prompts are excluded from Git. The set uses individual game-inspired art direction for each world, physical brush and print texture, and a clear central subject for small mobile cards. It does not use publisher art, logos, characters, or copied locations.

The 38 POI atlases and 38 destination markers are original `gpt-image-2` generations. Each world has its own prompt, material, color system, framing, and destination motif. The shipped atlas contains 49 live-place symbols; its destination marker is a separate full-resolution asset. Source prompts are kept in [`tools/imagegen/poi-atlases-all.jsonl`](../tools/imagegen/poi-atlases-all.jsonl); [`import_imagegen_poi_atlases.py`](../scripts/import_imagegen_poi_atlases.py) removes the chroma background and creates the app-sized sprites. A visual crop review found neighboring artwork in 43 category cells across nine worlds. Those cells have been regenerated as isolated single-icon images from [`poi-cell-repairs.jsonl`](../tools/imagegen/poi-cell-repairs.jsonl) and imported by [`import_imagegen_poi_cell_repairs.py`](../scripts/import_imagegen_poi_cell_repairs.py). Night POI glows are generated one cell at a time so they cannot spill into adjacent symbols. No game screenshot or extracted game asset is used. The same high-contrast symbols are used against each world's day and night map styles; local-time switching applies to the map palette, materials, and layout.

The current onboarding uses compact world-specific place markers, a horse illustration and two rendered clips. The 27 separate world emblem stickers use [`world-emblem-prompts.jsonl`](world-emblem-prompts.jsonl), with a different motif and material direction for each theme. `tools/waypoint-motion/install_artwork_v2.py` installs the compressed world covers; `install_world_emblems.py` removes the emblem chroma background and preserves the painted outline. `prepare_public_assets.py` stages the few Android images needed to re-render Remotion clips; generated copies remain ignored. Earlier unused sticker and onboarding stills are excluded from the source tree.

The first onboarding Earth image is adapted from NASA Earth Observatory's [Blue Marble western hemisphere](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/the-blue-marble-true-color-global-imagery-at-1km-resolution/) image by Reto Stöckli and Robert Simmon. The rotating globe and its country geometry are rendered in Waypoint from geographic data; the globes and motion clips in `tools/waypoint-motion/` are Waypoint compositions.

There are 38 world-card map previews (27 WebP resources under `drawable-nodpi/` and 11 PNG resources under `drawable/`), captured at documented public locations in `WorldPreviewPlaces.kt`. They still show the earlier base map rendering. The debug-only `WorldPreviewCaptureActivity` can refresh them against the new styles: capture once for day, once with the `night` intent extra, and Dark Zone with the `hologram` extra; each variant gets a distinct output name. These captures have not been run in this environment. OpenFreeMap supplies OpenMapTiles vector features and OpenStreetMap geography; attribution is shown in Map info and each card identifies its city or landmark.

Referral choices use the recognizable Instagram, Reddit, YouTube, Google, GitHub, and Telegram vector marks from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org/); the Friend mark is a Material icon. These marks identify the named services and do not imply affiliation.

Game names identify the source of stylistic inspiration. Waypoint is independent of those games and their publishers.

The original-art descriptions above apply to the map styles, place symbols, destination markers, world illustrations and promotional compositions. Retained interface and onboarding images are separate from those descriptions.
