FIELD26.6RELEASE

Smoother motion on car displays

On devices marked as low-RAM or with 4 GB of total system memory or less, Waypoint paces map animation at 30 frames per second. The lower update rate reduces rendering pressure on aftermarket car displays and other constrained hardware.

Keep motion moving.

Map frame scheduling adapts to memory-constrained devices, smoothing repeated updates without changing the normal frame rate on other hardware.

30 FPS · PACED

The reported 4 GB aftermarket unit ran Waypoint directly. Adaptive pacing now reduces map-rendering work on low-memory devices.

Zoom where you choose.

Manual gestures now take control of the map camera. A GPS update no longer takes that control back when your pinch ends.

No more snap-back.

Zooming, panning or rotating pauses automatic location-following, so you can inspect another part of the map without it jumping to your current fix.

Follow returns gently.

During active guidance, automatic camera-follow resumes after an eight-second grace period. While browsing, it waits until you choose to recenter.
