FIELD27.0RELEASE

Recognize a place at a glance.

Phone captures showed that map symbols were too small to make out at everyday zoom. Their rendered size is now lifted across all 38 worlds while each world keeps its own artwork.

More room for the mark.

Landmark symbols are about 20% larger. Nearby search results scale with zoom so their artwork stays easy to recognize beside themed places. Original day and night artwork stays unchanged.

Zoom where you choose.

Speed can change how far the navigation camera looks ahead. Its chosen 2D or 3D pitch stays steady, so the map no longer tilts by itself as speed changes.

Smoother motion on car displays.

The camera now holds its requested tilt while zoom changes with speed or a turn. The map stops pitching up and down on its own as pace changes.

One camera policy across worlds.

All map themes use the same stable pitch behavior. Switch between 2D and 3D whenever you want to change the view.

Speed belongs on the wide screen too.

In landscape navigation, the current speed and posted limit now sit below the compact turn instruction, where they remain visible while guidance continues.

Stopped still has a speed.

With a ready GPS fix, the readout shows 0 mph or 0 km/h at a standstill. A missing fix stays a dash, and the limit sign appears when a posted limit is available.
